Script in. Video out.

Three steps, and every step happens on your device.

1

Paste your script

Drishvana splits it into scenes. Add a headline, an image, a video clip or a gradient to each one.

2

Give it a voice

Record with your mic, upload narration, or generate a local AI voice (beta). Scene length follows the audio.

3

Export MP4

Word-by-word captions and music that ducks under your voice are burned in. Choose 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.

Built for long-form

How it differs

Drishvana is a composer, not a generator. It assembles your voice, text and media into a finished video.
